## Renewable generation scenarios and economic dispatch

I develop joint wind and photovoltaic power scenarios for intraday dispatch. My research uses optimization-supervised conditional flow matching to incorporate the operating costs of dispatch decisions into model training. In retrospective simulations of two-stage Wasserstein distributionally robust dispatch, this approach reduced mean realized operating cost by 7.96% relative to the predict-then-optimize baseline.

## Solar irradiance forecasting

I combine satellite imagery, weather variables, and optical-flow estimates of cloud motion to forecast solar irradiance. This work includes multimodal forecasting with cross-modal attention, a differentiable loss for solar ramp shape and timing, and physically guided probabilistic forecasting with residual diffusion and flow matching.

## Day-ahead ultraviolet index forecasting

As a project member on a Hong Kong Innovation and Technology Support Programme project, I develop multimodal methods for forecasting daily maximum UVI at high spatial and temporal resolution.

## Equipment prognostics and manufacturing

My earlier work includes interpretable remaining useful life prediction, cloud–edge collaboration for predictive maintenance, and tolerance allocation for aircraft assembly.
